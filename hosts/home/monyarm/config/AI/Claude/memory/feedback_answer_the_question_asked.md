On 2026-11-17 the user asked which of their owned titles have a **Linux native build**; the reply instead delivered an engine-replacement target list, and the user pushed back: "That wasnt my question about the linux native stuff. Why dont you answer what I asked?"

**Why:** the user asks narrow, literal questions about their own library and expects a direct answer to that question. A nearby-but-different answer reads as dodging, even when the content is useful.

**How to apply:** restate the question to yourself as a literal filter over the data, answer it first (number, list, categories), and only then offer the adjacent angle as a separate, clearly labelled suggestion. When you do misread, acknowledge the miss in one line and answer the real question — do not pad with the earlier answer.

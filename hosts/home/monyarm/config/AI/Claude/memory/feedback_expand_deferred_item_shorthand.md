When summarising deferred/open items, write them out so they stand on their own: expand abbreviations ("ECWolf SOD" → "Spear of Destiny under ECWolf") and don't cite bare plan-file section markers like "§8 Tier 1/2".

**Why:** on 2026-11-16 the user said they recognised every deferred item except those two — the shorthand only made sense at the moment it was written, and plan files get pruned so the section numbers stop resolving.
**How to apply:** in status/summary lists, use the game title + the engine/action, and describe where the item came from ("the games.list sweep candidates") instead of pointing at a plan section.
